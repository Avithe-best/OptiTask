Budget Calendar — Act Accordingly

  • Multi-site or enterprise deals require board approval — qualify early on ownership structure

Phase 5 · GTM — Competitive Defensibility

Why the Price Band Won't Stay Empty

Why Incumbents Can't Follow Us Down-Market

🚧

Ignition

$3,200/yr floor is structural, not strategic. A cheap SaaS tier would cannibalize their SI channel economics — integrators earn margin on license sales. Inductive Automation won't do it.

🚧

ThingsBoard

Open-source project with no hardware operations capability and no LoRa deployment infrastructure. Adding turnkey private LoRa would require a different company.

🚧

Ubidots

Cloud-native and developer-first by architecture. Cannot serve offline rural sites. Positioned for OEMs and devs, not plant operators. Different product category.

🚧

Xylem / Sensus

NaaS model requires owning RF network infrastructure. Cannot serve a 20-site operator without building a network first. Structurally locked to enterprise-scale deployments.

Modulink's Actual Moat

📡

Private LoRa — No Internet, No Carrier

Cloud-native competitors are architecturally excluded from offline rural sites. This is a structural barrier, not a feature advantage.

🏛️

On-Prem Data Sovereignty

Municipal IT departments often require local data. Cloud-only vendors structurally cannot offer it. Modulink's self-hosted model is a compliance feature, not a tradeoff.

🔌

Sensor Switching Cost

Once 4-20mA and Modbus sensors are wired to Modulink nodes, replacement means real labor, downtime, and rewiring. A meaningful cost to churn.

🤝

SI Network Effects

Trained SIs earning 15–25% hardware margin and reseller SaaS revenue do not switch. Each SI adds deployment lock-in across their entire customer portfolio.
