  • Modular Skills — start simple, expand over time

Phase 5 · GTM — E-Commerce Store

Selling Hardware Directly Off the Website

Platform

Shopify

$79–$299/mo. Handles inventory, shipping, payments, and integrates with 3PL fulfillment partners. Add Stripe for SaaS subscription checkout in the same flow.

Store Structure

3 Must-Haves

  • Use-case landing pages ("For Pump Stations," "For RO Systems") — serve SEO and buyer self-qualification
  • Product pages with wiring diagrams, sensor compatibility tables, IP ratings — operators won't buy without specs
  • SaaS subscription bundled at checkout as an add-on (auto-activate onboarding email)
Conversion Levers

Reduce Friction

  • Free 30-day hardware demo kit (loaner) → convert on SaaS subscription
  • Free shipping above $500 to cut cart abandonment
  • 30-day no-questions return policy (standard expectation for industrial electronics buyers)
  • Quickstart card in every box pointing to hosted docs
Fulfillment

What's Needed

  • Hardware assembly / kitting location (in-house or contract manufacturer)
  • 3PL (ShipBob or similar) once volume exceeds ~20–30 units/month
  • Standard UPS/FedEx ground — hardware is non-hazmat electronics
  • Verify EAR99 export classification on LoRa radio hardware before international orders
SaaS ↔ Hardware Flywheel

The Loop

Hardware purchase → auto-trigger SaaS trial activation → operator sees value in 30 days → converts to Pro or Plant subscription → expansion as they add more nodes → renewal.

Hardware is the acquisition vehicle. SaaS is the business.
